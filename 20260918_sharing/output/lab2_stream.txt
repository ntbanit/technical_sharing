# 5 Uses for Claude Agents in Customer Support

1. **Instant Ticket Triage and Routing**
   - Automatically categorize incoming support requests by issue type, urgency, and required expertise
   - Route tickets to the appropriate team or specialist, reducing response times

2. **First-Level Problem Resolution**
   - Answer common questions about products, policies, billing, and account management
   - Provide troubleshooting steps and self-service solutions without human intervention

3. **Knowledge Base Search and Synthesis**
   - Query your support documentation in natural language to find relevant answers
   - Combine multiple sources to provide comprehensive responses to complex questions

4. **Proactive Issue Detection**
   - Analyze customer messages to identify escalating frustration or urgent problems
   - Flag high-priority issues for immediate human review before they become critical

5. **Context-Aware Handoff to Humans**
   - Gather relevant information and summarize customer issues before transferring to support staff
   - Provide agents with conversation history and key details to enable faster, more informed assistance

These applications help reduce support costs while improving response times and customer satisfaction.
